# dd214_discharge_differs (cfile)

The veteran says their discharge was honorable, but the DD-214 in the C-file says general (under honorable conditions). The DD-214 wins on discharge - prefill records general and a staff task notes the difference (scored in the cfile condition). General is not a stop rule, so the case stays eligible and reaches stage 5 (ready for attorney review).

Behaviours: If asked how you heard about the firm, say you found it on the internet; say where only when asked where., You are sure your discharge was honorable and say so plainly when asked, without hedging. Don't bring up your DD-214. If the assistant tells you the DD-214 shows a different discharge, say you didn't know that and it may well be right.
