# Close the gaps (intake pass 2)

Claims prep has pre-filled what the C-file settles (questions marked `cfile: true`: rated
conditions, MOS, the earlier representative) and listed what the C-file contradicts. Pass 2 asks:
1. any pass-1 question that became relevant (e.g. the C-file shows an old claim, so the rating
   questions now apply);
2. in confirm mode, one check of what the C-file contradicts ("Your VA claims file shows ...");
3. the remaining questions on the firm's form, then the 16 narrative prompts per condition.
